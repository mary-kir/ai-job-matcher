# AI Job Matcher

AI Job Matcher is a planned personal web app for finding and evaluating Product
Manager and Product Owner vacancies against a candidate’s CV and career
preferences, then tracking saved, rejected and applied jobs.

The initial focus is English-speaking Junior, Associate and relevant non-senior
roles in Dublin and elsewhere in Europe, including relocation and visa-support
considerations.

Planned vacancy intake includes permitted browser-based extraction with
Playwright, individual job URLs and manual text input. The product will not
depend solely on job-board APIs.

## Project Status

Product definition only. No application code has been implemented, and product
assumptions have not yet been validated.

See [the product brief](docs/product-brief.md) for scope, priorities, proposed
success metrics, risks and roadmap.
